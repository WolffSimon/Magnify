# Magnify runbook

The order of operations for any document an AI drafts for a person to send, publish or sign.

1. **Settle the target.** Write down the document, its limits, who reads it first, and what it must do for them. Where there is a brief, a posting or a request, that is the target. Where there is none, write a short target file.
2. **Ask first** (`magnify-ask-first`). Test the request: would a hundred writers produce a hundred near-identical documents from it? If so, it is thin. Check the record, then ask numbered questions for what is missing: the reader, the purpose, the facts, the author's view, what must not be said, the material and the limits. Stop and wait for the answers. If the author says "just write it", draft with visible placeholders and invent nothing.
3. **Read the law** (`magnify-prose-law`) in full, with the author's own list of rulings. Then draft.
4. **Check** (`magnify-slop-check`). Fix every fault, or keep a one-line reason. Look at the advisories.
5. **Cold review** (`magnify-cold-review`). Use a new reviewer each round. Triage every finding. Stop when no high or medium finding remains, or after three rounds.
6. **Hand over.** Give the author four things:
   - the document
   - the scores round by round
   - the decisions only they can make, numbered
   - the findings kept against the reviewers, each with a reason
7. **Learn.** When the author corrects a draft, add the correction to their list of rulings and the pattern to their bans file, with the date and their words. The next draft starts from the corrected law.
